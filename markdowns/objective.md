# Objectives

An review above reveals rich dynamics for low Reynolds number flows over airfoils and the growing share of data-driven techniques to study complex flow physics. The aim of this study is to develop reduced-order model of the pitching airfoil that remains as faithful as possible to the physics while retaining as few degrees of freedom as possible, so as to isolate the dominant flow physics and enable real time control.  

- **Characterize the Flow Physics** of a pitching airfoil at low Reynolds numbers, with particular attention to the formation and evolution of dynamically important structures such as leading-edge vortices.

- **Identify a low-dimensional representation** of the flow from high-fidelity numerical data using data-driven dimensionality-reduction techniques.

- **Develop a Reduced-Order Model** to predict the aerodynamic response for a given pitching input. We seek to employ data-driven techniques discussed above while simultaneously remaining attentive to the dominant flow physics such as leading-edge vortices

- **Assessing the Suitability and Robustness** of the developed ROM as it concerns flow control.
